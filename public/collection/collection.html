THE CURATED COLLECTION

Beyond
Appearance.

A gemstone is more than the light it reflects. Each stone is the result of geological processes that unfolded across immense periods of time.

The collection presented here focuses on natural gemstones selected for their individuality, character and connection to the island of Ceylon.

01

NATURAL SAPPHIRE

Ceylon
Blue Sapphire.

Among the gemstones for which Sri Lanka is renowned, blue sapphire occupies a particularly distinguished place.

Its colour can range from delicate cornflower tones to deeper blue expressions, with each stone possessing its own individual character.

Formed through geological processes long before human history, every natural sapphire carries a fragment of that ancient story.

02

NATURAL SAPPHIRE

Padparadscha
Sapphire.

Among the rarest sapphire colours, Padparadscha is recognised for its delicate balance of pink and orange.

The name itself has become associated with one of the most distinctive colour expressions found in the world of natural sapphire.

Each stone reveals its own subtle relationship between warmth, light and colour.

03

NATURAL SAPPHIRE

Ceylon
Yellow Sapphire.

Ceylon yellow sapphires are known for their distinctive warmth, ranging from soft golden tones to richer expressions of yellow.

Like every natural gemstone, its colour is the result of the particular geological conditions under which the crystal formed.

What makes each stone compelling is not uniformity, but individuality.
